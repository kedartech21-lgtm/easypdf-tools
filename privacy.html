Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview & 100% Client-Side Processing

EasyPDF ("we", "our", or "us") is built with a strict focus on user privacy. All PDF processing tasks—including merging, splitting, compressing, rotating, converting images, and reordering pages—occur entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript libraries (`pdf-lib` and `pdf.js`). Your files, documents, and personal data are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external server or third party.

2. Analytics

We use privacy-friendly web analytics provided by Vercel to measure general aggregate traffic, page views, and performance metrics. These analytics do not track individual users across the web and operate without intrusive tracking cookies.

3. Third-Party Advertisements (Google AdSense)

We may display advertisements through third-party vendors, such as Google AdSense, to support the maintenance and free availability of EasyPDF.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out via our repository or support channels.
